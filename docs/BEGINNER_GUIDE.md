# Complete Beginner's Guide (No Coding or AI Agent Experience Needed)

If this is your first time using **Antigravity (AGY)** and you have never touched a terminal, Python, or Git, follow these simple steps.

---

## Step 1: Install Basic Tools on Windows

1. **Antigravity (AGY):** Make sure Antigravity is installed and you are signed in.
2. **Python (used to assemble your formatted Word document):**
   - Download the latest Python 3.10+ installer from <https://www.python.org/downloads/>.
   - **IMPORTANT during setup:** On the very first installer screen, check **"Add Python to PATH"** before clicking *Install Now*.
3. **Zotero Desktop (for live Word citations & Bibliography):**
   - Download and install from <https://www.zotero.org/download/> (automatically adds the *Zotero* tab to Microsoft Word).
   - Open Zotero -> **Edit -> Settings (Preferences) -> Advanced -> General** -> check **"Allow other applications on this computer to communicate with Zotero"**.
4. **Microsoft Word:** Used at the final step to open the generated `.docx` file and click **Zotero -> Refresh**.

---

## Step 2: Open This Folder in Antigravity

1. If you downloaded a `.zip` file, right-click it -> **Extract All...** into your workspace folder (e.g., `D:\My_Thesis\`).
   If you used Git, clone the repository to `D:\My_Thesis\`.
2. Open **Antigravity (AGY)**.
3. Click **File -> Open Folder...** and select `D:\My_Thesis\` (you should see `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and `01. Literature/` inside).

---

## Step 3: Start Chatting with AGY

You **never need to memorize commands**. Open the AGY chat panel and send whichever message matches your starting point:

* **If you have zero idea what to write about yet:**
  > *"Hi, I'm a PKN STAN student and I have no idea what topic to write my thesis on yet. Please check my setup and guide me from zero."*

* **If you already have a rough area of interest or PKL/KTTA topic:**
  > *"Hi, I'm in the [your study program] program at PKN STAN and previously wrote a KTTA / interned on [topic]. Please suggest realistic thesis topics with accessible data that comply with PER-9/PKN/2020."*

* **If your advisor already approved a title:**
  > *"Hi, my thesis title is already approved: '[your title]'. Please check my setup and help me start gathering literature and building the blueprint."*

---

## Tips for Working Smoothly with AGY

1. **Always speak up if anything sounds too technical:** Just say *"Explain that in simple words"* or *"Pick the best option for me."*
2. **Never invent numbers or PDFs:** Put your journal PDFs, regulations (PMK/UU/PER), and Excel datasets inside `01. Literature/` and `02. Data & Analysis/`, then tell AGY the filenames.
3. **Prepare 1 past writing sample (Phase 3):** Drop 1–2 of your own previous works (such as your Diploma III KTTA or a course paper) into `05. Voice Reference/` so AGY learns your natural writing rhythm rather than sounding robotic.
4. **Pick up anytime:** Progress is automatically saved in `PROJECT_STATUS.md`. In any new chat, simply say *"Continue my thesis progress"* to resume right where you stopped.
