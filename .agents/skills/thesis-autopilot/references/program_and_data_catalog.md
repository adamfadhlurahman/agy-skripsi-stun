# PKN STAN Study Programs, `PER-9/PKN/2020` Archetypes & Indonesian Open Data Catalog

Reference guide for AGY during **Phase 0 & Phase 1A** when guiding PKN STAN students who are starting from scratch ("from minus").

---

## 1. PKN STAN Study Program & Topic Mapping

| Department / Study Program | Popular & Feasible Thesis Themes |
|---|---|
| **D-IV Akuntansi Sektor Publik (ASP)** | Tax expenditure evaluation, central/local government financial accounting (LKPP/LKPD/PSAP), public-sector auditing & BPK findings, value-for-money analysis of APBN/TKDD programs, ESG/carbon accounting & energy transition, BLU/BUMN governance, government accounting information systems. |
| **D-IV Manajemen Keuangan Negara (MKN)** | APBN fiscal effectiveness & SBN/SBSN debt financing, ministerial spending reviews, intergovernmental fiscal transfers (DAU, DAK, DBH, Dana Desa), macro-fiscal policy, tax/PNBP revenue performance, treasury & public cash management (KPPN). |
| **D-IV Manajemen Aset Publik (MAP)** | State/regional asset optimization & valuation (BMN/BMD), asset utilization partnerships (KSP/BGS/KPBU), lease/auction/disposal efficiency, government property & LMAN management, infrastructure economic impact evaluation. |
| **Other / Transfer Programs (Taxation, Customs & Excise, Valuation, Treasury)** | Tax compliance & tax gap modeling, VAT/income tax policy incentives, customs/excise revenue & enforcement, property/business/natural-resource valuation, digital Ministry of Finance public services. |

## 2. Four Official Thesis Archetypes (`PER-9/PKN/2020` Art. 3.1.2.1)

Help the student select **1 archetype** matching their data and strengths:

1. **Quantitative (Descriptive or Causal / Econometrics)**
   - *Best if:* Comfortable with numbers, panel/DiD/IV regressions, or structured quantitative evaluation using secondary data or surveys.
   - *Chapters III & IV Titles:* `METODE PENELITIAN` -> `HASIL PENELITIAN DAN PEMBAHASAN`.
2. **Qualitative (Case Study or Non-Case Study)**
   - *Best if:* Has interview/observation access at an office (KPP, KPPN, Bea Cukai, KPKNL, Pemda, BPK, BLU) or analyzes policy implementation deeply.
   - *Chapters III & IV Titles:* `METODE PENELITIAN` -> `HASIL PENELITIAN DAN PEMBAHASAN`.
3. **Descriptive Simulation**
   - *Best if:* Evaluates policy/tariff/incentive scenarios against revenue, cost, or organizational performance outcomes.
   - *Chapters III & IV Titles:* `INTI PENELITIAN` -> `HASIL PENELITIAN DAN EVALUASI`.
4. **Descriptive Design (Systems / Prototype Engineering)**
   - *Best if:* Designs an accounting information system prototype, business-process workflow, internal control dashboard, or application architecture for an institution.
   - *Chapters III & IV Titles:* `ANALISIS SISTEM YANG BERJALAN` -> `RANCANGAN SISTEM YANG DIUSULKAN`.

## 3. Indonesian Open Public Data Sources (No Lengthy Permits Required)

Use this catalog when a student says *"I don't have access to internal office data"*:

| Data Source | Available Data | Access Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| **Kemenkeu APBN & Fiscal Reports** (`data-apbn.kemenkeu.go.id`, Nota Keuangan, LKPP, Tax Expenditure Report) | APBN realization, ministerial spending, subsidies, tax expenditure estimates by tax type & sector. | **Very Easy** (Public PDF/Excel) |
| **DJPK Kemenkeu** (`djpk.kemenkeu.go.id/portal/data/apbd`) | Local government budgets (APBD) across all provinces/districts, PAD, DAU, DAK, DBH, Dana Desa. | **Very Easy** (Panel-ready CSV/Excel) |
| **BPS Statistics Indonesia** (`bps.go.id`) | Regional GDP (PDRB), poverty, HDI, inflation, labor, industry/energy/transport indicators. | **Very Easy** (Tables/API) |
| **IDX / BEI & Annual Reports** (`idx.co.id`) | Audited financial statements, ESG/sustainability disclosures, deferred tax, effective tax rates, SOE governance. | **Very Easy** (Public PDF) |
| **BPK RI** (`bpk.go.id` — IHPS & LHP) | Audit opinions, SPI & compliance findings counts and values across central/local entities. | **Easy** (Public PDF) |
| **OJK & Bank Indonesia** (`ojk.go.id`, `bi.go.id`) | Banking/fintech statistics, exchange rates, interest rates, inflation, balance of payments. | **Very Easy** (Public Excel/PDF) |
| **JDIH Kemenkeu & BPK** (`jdih.kemenkeu.go.id`, `peraturan.bpk.go.id`) | Full texts of UU, PP, Perpres, PMK, and Director General decrees for policy timelines. | **Very Easy** (Public PDF) |
| **LKPP Procurement & International Data** (`sirup.lkpp.go.id`, GAIKINDO, ESDM/PLN, World Bank, IMF, IEA) | Public procurement data, sectoral energy/automotive data, cross-country macro indicators. | **Easy** (Public) |

## 4. Traffic-Light Feasibility Matrix for Beginners

Rate every candidate topic honestly before locking Gate 1:
* **GREEN (Ready to Execute):** 100% public data or already in student's hands; method matches skillset; solid literature base; realistically finishes within one semester.
* **YELLOW (Needs Access Confirmation):** Requires formal research permission letters or interviews; must include a fallback contingency plan in the Proposal (`PER-9` Art. 2.4).
* **RED (High Risk — Avoid on Tight Deadlines):** Confidential microdata (e.g., individual taxpayer returns without official DJP research clearance), unreleased period data, or fieldwork exceeding the academic calendar.
